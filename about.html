Our Story

Rooted in Engineering, Driven by Impact

OpenSoftwares was founded with an uncompromising belief: Indian institutions and enterprises deserve world-class software tailored to their regional realities, compliance mandates, and operational rhythms.

Transforming Education First with Schoolmate

We began by observing the chaotic reality of school administration across Maharashtra. Teachers spent hours filling attendance registers by hand, parents queued outside administrative offices to pay term fees, and guardians worried anxiously about school bus delays on congested roads.

We engineered Schoolmate from scratch to eliminate this friction. By pairing an intuitive mobile application for parents with a rock-solid, multi-branch ERP for school trustees, Schoolmate has brought transparency, safety, and operational calm to hundreds of campuses.

As OpenSoftwares expands into manufacturing systems and real estate PropTech, our foundational ethos remains unchanged: building robust systems that perform reliably day in and day out.
